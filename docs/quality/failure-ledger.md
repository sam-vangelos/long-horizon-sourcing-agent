# Failure Ledger

Append new entries at the top of the table. Keep entries concise, specific, and
safe to share across agents. Do not include secrets, raw candidate data, or raw
conversation transcripts.

## Entry fields

- **ID**: stable short id, e.g. `INTAKE-2026-05-17-01`.
- **Date**: discovery date.
- **Flow**: Tier-0 flow or surface affected.
- **Boundary**: state, routing, prompt, API, persistence, build, package, auth,
  test-model, UX language, or ownership.
- **Symptom**: what the user or test saw.
- **Violated invariant**: link to an invariant heading when available.
- **Root cause**: the smallest concrete cause known so far.
- **Detection gap**: why existing tests/logs did not catch it earlier.
- **Regression / guard**: test, script, or runtime guard added.
- **Owner**: owning boundary or person.
- **Status**: open, guarded, fixed, or known-failure.

- repeated violated invariant
- repeated boundary

- failures caused by duplicated source of truth

the largest repeated class.
