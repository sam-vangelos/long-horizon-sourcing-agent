# Boolean Query Scoring, Lexicon Enrichment, and Sidecar Integration

## Summary

LinkedIn Recruiter search strings authored by the strategy seat (`linkedin/strategy.py`) pass through an opt-in, two-stage Boolean quality pipeline before execution (`_finalize_execution_plan` at `linkedin/strategy.py:1247-1305`) and during mid-run query adaptation (`_annotate_adaptation_metadata` at `linkedin/strategy.py:707-723`). Every string is evaluated by a deterministic parser and multiplicative scorer (`linkedin/boolean_score/`): the rule-based lexicon enricher (`linkedin/boolean_enrichment.py`, `linkedin/boolean_score/enrich.py`) expands declared common forms and morphological/hyphen twins while verifying parse-structure preservation (`_structure_preserved`), and the local RL sidecar client (`linkedin/boolean_sidecar.py`) accepts a proposed rewrite only when the scorer verifies a strictly higher `log_composite` (`MIN_GAIN = 0.01`). The GRPO training pipeline, 158/49 stratified concept split, blind-rater calibration harness, and `Q8_0` GGUF sidecar server live in the companion repository [`boolean-search-rl`](https://github.com/sam-vangelos/boolean-search-rl).

## Deterministic Parser, Role Lexicon, and Multiplicative Scorer (`linkedin/boolean_score/`)

`linkedin/boolean_score/` evaluates a Boolean string in three steps without calling an LLM:

1. **Parser and LinkedIn Recruiter legality (`linkedin/boolean_score/parser.py`)**:
   - `parse(boolean)` tokenizes a string (`_tokenize`) into a `ParseResult` containing `source`, the operator `tree` (`Term`, `And`, `Or`, `Not`), `errors` (`tuple[ParseError, ...]` with `code`, `message`, `span`), `findings` (`tuple[Finding, ...]` with `code`, `severity`, `message`, `span`), and `max_depth`.
   - Hard syntax errors (`empty_input`, `unclosed_quote`, `unbalanced_parenthesis`, `dangling_operator`, `empty_group`) and illegal LinkedIn Recruiter operators (`malformed_operator` for `&&`, `||`, or bare `&`, `wildcard_operator` for `*` outside quotes via `mask_quoted_spans`, and `not_group_contains_and` via `contains_and`) emit `ParseError` entries, setting `ParseResult.legal = False`, `ScoreReport.composite = 0.0`, and `ScoreReport.log_composite = math.log(LOG_FLOOR)` (`-13.8155`, where `LOG_FLOOR = 1e-6`).
   - Nine recoverable structural defects emit `severity="warning"` `Finding` records (`_tokenize`, `_bare_run_findings`, and `_structure_findings` in `linkedin/boolean_score/score.py`) converted into `structure_charges` via `STRUCTURE_WEIGHTS`: `unquoted_multiword_run` (`0.10`), `lowercase_operator` (`0.20`), `noop_special_character` (`0.20`), `comma_numeral` (`0.10`), `mid_word_stem` (`0.20`), `nesting_depth_over_2` (`0.20`, `NESTING_LIMIT = 2`), `and_groups_over_3` (`0.15`, `AND_GATE_LIMIT = 2`), `sentence_fragment` (`0.15`), and `length_over_cap` (`0.30`, `LENGTH_CAP = 2000`).

2. **Concept lexicon (`linkedin/boolean_score/lexicon.py`)**:
   - `load_lexicon()` and `build_lexicon()` load `linkedin/boolean_score/lexicons/research-engineer-post-training.json` (`207` concepts for `research-engineer-post-training`) into an immutable `Lexicon`.
   - Each `Concept` specifies `id`, `families`, `kind` (`CLASS_KIND = "class"` with `@property def names_a_class` or `INSTANCE_KIND = "instance"`), `forms` (`tuple[Form, ...]`), `proper_noun`, `unsure`, and optional `relevance` (`"core"`, `"adjacent"`, `"peripheral"`), exposing `common_forms`, `occasional_forms`, and `rare_forms` across `COMMON_REGISTER = "common"`, `OCCASIONAL_REGISTER = "occasional"`, and `RARE_REGISTER = "rare"`.

3. **Multiplicative composite score (`linkedin/boolean_score/score.py`)**:
   - `score(boolean, lexicon, *, anchored_by_filter=False, discovery_not=False)` builds a `ScoredGroup` per `OR` group (`_groups_for`) and computes `ScoreReport`:
     $$\text{composite} = \text{parsimony} \times \text{SHAPE\_SCORES}[\text{shape}] \times \frac{\sum_{k \in \text{WEIGHTS}} w_k \cdot c_k}{\sum_{k} w_k}$$
   - **Coverage (`WEIGHTS`)**: `register_coverage` (`_register_coverage`, weight `0.30`, expected-form-weighted fraction of `lexicon.expected_forms(concept)` present plus `OCCASIONAL_CREDIT = 0.05` per `concept.occasional_forms` present, or `min(1.0, reached / min(SWEEP_TARGET, max(1, lexicon.instances_in_family(sweep))))` with `SWEEP_TARGET = 15` on pure instance sweeps via `_sweep_family`), `variant_coverage` (`_variant_coverage`, weight `0.15`, twin coverage over `lexicon.twins_for`), `cohesion` (`_cohesion` / `_group_cohesion`, weight `0.10`, dominant-family share with `PARENT_COHESION = 0.75`), and `relevance` (`_relevance`, weight `0.05`, `RELEVANCE_SCORES = {"core": 1.0, "adjacent": 0.7, "peripheral": 0.4}` and `UNMAPPED_RELEVANCE = 0.2`). When `register_coverage` or `variant_coverage` is `None` (`NEUTRAL_WHEN_INAPPLICABLE`), `score()` substitutes `NEUTRAL_SCORE = 0.20` for `register_coverage` and `lexicon.neutral_variant` for `variant_coverage`.
   - **Shape (`shape_of`, `SHAPE_SCORES`)**: `anchor_and_sweep` (`1.00`), `anchored_by_filter` (`1.00`), `lone_sweep` (`0.90`), `two_groups_no_anchor` (`0.85`), and `lone_anchor` (`0.75`).
   - **Parsimony (`_parsimony`, `BLOAT_COEFFICIENTS`, `PARSIMONY_DECAY = 1.5`)**: $\exp(-1.5 \times \text{bloat\_sum})$ across 12 bloat charges (`rare_form: 0.04` capped at `RARE_FORM_CAP = 0.30`, `repeated_concept: 0.25`, `generic_and_gate: 0.35`, `excess_not_term: 0.10` above `NOT_TERM_LIMIT = 4`, `grab_bag: 0.30` below `GRAB_BAG_THRESHOLD = 0.5`, `umbrella_and_gate: 0.75`, `employer_gate: 0.50`, `employer_facet_gate: 0.10`, `over_gated: 0.40`, `noisy_term: 0.10` capped at `NOISY_TERM_CAP = 0.30`, `self_exclusion: 0.30` capped at `SELF_EXCLUSION_CAP = 0.75`, `umbrella_in_group: 0.30`) plus `structure_charges`.

## Stage 1: Deterministic Lexicon Enrichment (`linkedin/boolean_enrichment.py`)

Controlled by `LINKEDIN_LEXICON_ENRICHMENT_ENABLED` (`shared/config.py:446-447`, default `False`; lexicon path overridable via `LINKEDIN_BOOLEAN_LEXICON_PATH` at `shared/config.py:450`), `enrich_plan(plan)` (`linkedin/boolean_enrichment.py:119-124`) and `enrich_adaptation(adaptation)` (`linkedin/boolean_enrichment.py:127-131`) run `enrich()` from `linkedin/boolean_score/enrich.py` over `generated_strings[].boolean`, `coverage_gaps[].suggested_boolean`, and `new_strings[].boolean`.

`enrich(boolean, lexicon, *, max_chars=DEFAULT_MAX_CHARS)` in `linkedin/boolean_score/enrich.py` (`DEFAULT_MAX_CHARS = 2000`) returns an `EnrichReport` (`original`, `enriched`, `legal`, `changes: tuple[GroupChange, ...]`, `truncated`, `rejected`, `@property def unchanged`):
- Rewrites only positive, contiguous `OR` groups (`not negated`, `_terms_are_contiguous`, `_closes_safely`), never modifying `NOT` exclusions or operator structure.
- `_candidates_for(group, lexicon, present)` expands missing `concept.common_forms` and morphology/hyphen twins (`lexicon.twins_for`, skipping `concept.proper_noun`) to a fixed point (`enrich(enrich(x)) == enrich(x)`), while `_may_add()` screens out terms that `_would_be_charged()`, `lexicon.is_umbrella()`, `lexicon.is_noisy()`, `lexicon.is_generic_verb()`, `lexicon.is_employer()`, `RARE_REGISTER`, or owned by a concept outside the group.
- `_removable_rare()` drops authored `RARE_REGISTER` forms once sibling or newly added common forms cover the same concept, and `_structure_preserved(before, after, lexicon, dropped)` verifies that the re-parsed AST preserves group structure.
- `enrich_items()` (`linkedin/boolean_enrichment.py:67-102`) records `<field>_original`, attaches `<field>_enrichment` (`report.to_dict()`), and stamps `boolean_score` (`_score_summary`).

## Stage 2: Local RL Sidecar Client and Anti-Padding Gates (`linkedin/boolean_sidecar.py`)

The deterministic enricher cannot expand a term that is missing from the lexicon, splice synonyms into a thin run inside a multi-concept group, or invent a class anchor for a `lone_sweep` query. When `LINKEDIN_ENRICHER_SIDECAR_ENABLED` (`shared/config.py:458-459`, default `False`) is enabled, `enrich_plan_with_sidecar(plan)` (`linkedin/boolean_sidecar.py:860-870`) and `enrich_adaptation_with_sidecar(adaptation)` (`linkedin/boolean_sidecar.py:873-877`) query the local `boolean-search-rl` HTTP sidecar (`LINKEDIN_ENRICHER_SIDECAR_URL`, default `"http://127.0.0.1:8765"`, timeout `LINKEDIN_ENRICHER_SIDECAR_TIMEOUT_SECONDS = 90.0`, `LINKEDIN_ENRICHER_SIDECAR_SAMPLES = 8`).

`_improve(boolean, lexicon, ask)` (`linkedin/boolean_sidecar.py:700-780`) runs three candidate-generation passes in sequence:
1. **Whole-group `register` pass (`REGISTER_TASK = "register"`)**: for each positive `OR` group that is unmapped or maps to a single concept (`_single_concept`), requests `n = 8` candidate groups from `POST /enrich` and evaluates full-group replacements (`_register_trials`).
2. **Concept-run `register` splicing (`REGISTER_RUN_TASK = "register_run"`)**: for multi-concept positive `OR` groups, partitions the group into contiguous concept runs (`_concept_runs`), selects up to `MAX_CONCEPT_RUNS_PER_STRING = 3` thin runs with fewer than `RICH_RUN_FORMS = 4` forms (`_chosen_runs`), queries `POST /enrich` (`task="register"`) on the sub-group, and splices new surface forms immediately after the run (`_run_trials`) without removing or reordering authored terms.
3. **`anchor` prepayment (`ANCHOR_TASK = "anchor"`)**: if the query still has `current.shape == LONE_SWEEP_SHAPE` (`"lone_sweep"`) and `not current.anchor_present`, queries `POST /enrich` (`task="anchor"`) on the sweep group and prepends `<proposal> AND <current>` (`_anchor_trials`).

Every candidate string produced by `_register_trials`, `_run_trials`, or `_anchor_trials` must pass six client-side gates before `_best()` (`linkedin/boolean_sidecar.py:680-697`) can accept it:
- **Exact and near-duplicate rejection (`_deduped`, `_padded`)**: rejects normalized duplicate terms and unmapped morphological padding within edit distance `NEAR_DUPLICATE_DISTANCE = 2` on strings of at least `NEAR_DUPLICATE_MIN_LENGTH = 5` characters (`_edit_distance`), as well as space/hyphen-fused compounds (`_run_together`, `_fused`).
- **Lexical and concept root preservation (`_off_root`, `_drops_a_qualifier`, `_unrooted_anchor`)**: on mapped groups, rejects additions that map to a different concept or that fail to share a word stem (`_stem`, `_stems`, `_rooted`) with the authored terms; on unmapped terms, forbids dropping a qualifier stem from an authored phrase (`_word_stems`).
- **Structural and length preservation (`_signature`, `DEFAULT_MAX_CHARS = 2000`)**: verifies that every untouched `OR` group and `NOT` exclusion retains its exact normalized term tuple and that total length stays within `2000` characters (`_acceptable`).
- **Keep-only-if-scorer-improves (`MIN_GAIN = 0.01`)**: `_best()` accepts a candidate **only if** `report.log_composite > current.log_composite + MIN_GAIN` (`0.01`), breaking ties within `MIN_GAIN` by fewest unmapped terms (`unmapped`) and shortest character length.
- **Circuit breaker (`_Breaker`, `_SidecarDown`)**: if `request_candidates()` fails twice on connection refusal or timeout (`Candidates(failed=True)`), `breaker.tripped = True` halts sidecar requests for the rest of the plan or adaptation and preserves the deterministic string.

## Companion Repository (`boolean-search-rl`) and Evaluation Tooling

Offline replay and corpus-scoring tools in `tools/` exercise this pipeline without a browser:
- `tools/score_boolean_corpus.py`: scores a JSON corpus (`tests/fixtures/boolean_corpus/ml_runs_76.json` and `tests/fixtures/boolean_corpus/adversarial.json`) against `linkedin/boolean_score/`.
- `tools/score_enrichment.py`: runs deterministic lexicon enrichment across the `76`-string corpus (`tests/fixtures/boolean_corpus/ml_runs_76.json`) and `52` blind-rated targets (`tests/fixtures/boolean_corpus/rater_targets.json`).
- `tools/sidecar_replay.py`: replays `linkedin.boolean_sidecar._improve` (`plan_strings`, `PLAN_FILE = "execution_plan.json"`, `SURFACES`) against a live sidecar endpoint without mutating stored plans.

The companion repository `boolean-search-rl` packages the vendored scorer (`linkedin/boolean_score/`), the `158`/`49` stratified A/B concept split (`SEED = 20260921`, `HOLDOUT_SHARE = 0.25`), the `244`-prompt dataset (`189` training, `55` held-out evaluation), the gated GRPO reward functions (`register` and `anchor`), the `Qwen/Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct` LoRA training and held-out evaluation results, and the `Q8_0` GGUF export and `llama-server` Metal HTTP sidecar (`GET /health`, `POST /enrich`).
